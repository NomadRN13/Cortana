Privacy Policy

Your data, plainly.

Effective August 2026 · Applies to the 40/LOVE app and 40love website

Founder note: this policy is drafted to match exactly what the product does today. Have a licensed attorney review it before public launch.

Who we are

40/LOVE is a dating and social app for racquet sports players, based in Indianapolis, Indiana. Questions about this policy: hello@40love.app.

What we collect

  • How you sign in: you can use an emailed one-time code, Sign in with Apple, or Continue with Google. If you use Apple or Google, we receive only your name and email address from them — nothing else, and nothing for advertising. Apple lets you hide your real address behind a private relay address, and that works fine here. One thing worth being clear about: whichever provider you choose necessarily learns that you signed in to 40/LOVE, and 40/LOVE is a dating app. If you'd rather no one else know that, use the emailed code instead.
  • Account: your email address (used to sign you in with a one-time code — we never store a password), your phone number (verified once by text when you join, so every member is a real person; it is never shown to other members, never used for marketing, and used only to prove that verification and to secure your account — and it is deleted with everything else if you delete your account), first name, and birthdate (to enforce our 18+ rule; your birthdate is never shown to other members — only your age).
  • Profile: the sports you play and skill level, gender and matching preferences (dating, play, and friends), photos you add, your bio and availability.
  • Approximate location: rounded to about 1 kilometer on your device before it is sent. Your exact location never reaches our servers — we can't show it, leak it, or sell it, because we never have it. Other members are never given a position at all, rounded or otherwise: the app tells them how far away you are, and the distance is worked out on our side. The same goes for your birthdate — other members can only ever be told your age.
  • Your devices: when you sign in on a phone we record a name for it (usually whatever you've called it, like "Sam's iPhone"), whether it's an iPhone or an Android, and when it last opened the app — so you can see the list in Settings and sign out a phone you've lost or no longer trust. We do not read any hardware serial or advertising identifier; the app makes up a random tag for itself, and uninstalling erases it. If you turn on notifications, that phone also gets a push token, which is deleted the moment you sign the device out.
  • Activity: likes and passes, matches, messages you send, events you join, and reports or blocks you file.
  • The waitlist, if you join it: on the website, before the app is open in your area, we take your email address and the city you picked — nothing else, and it is kept separate from app accounts. We use it once, to tell you when 40/LOVE opens near you. Nobody can use the site to test whether a given address is on the list — asking to join an address that is already on it tells the asker nothing at all. We can see our own list, because otherwise we could not email you when your city opens. Email us and we'll take you off it.
  • Visits to our website: our web host (Netlify) keeps the ordinary server log every website has — which page was asked for, when, the rough location it came from, and the IP address that asked. We read those logs only as totals: how many people came, which pages they opened, which links sent them. There is no script in the page doing this, no cookie is set, nothing is stored on your device, and the data never leaves our host or gets attached to a 40/LOVE account. It also cannot follow you anywhere else — a server can only ever see its own visitors.
  • How the demo is used, anonymously: the clickable demo on our website counts how far people get — opened it, started signing up, finished — so we can see where the flow loses people and fix it. What we store is a step name and a random number the page invents when it loads and forgets when you leave. No account, no cookie, no IP address, nothing that survives the visit, and nothing that could be traced back to you. The app on your phone does not do this at all.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We don't run advertising or ad tracking of any kind.
  • We don't collect your last name, contacts, or precise GPS location.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

To run the product: showing your profile to compatible members, matching by sport, skill, distance, and preferences, delivering messages, running events, sending the notifications you've enabled, and keeping the community safe (reviewing reports, enforcing blocks and bans).

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the product — our database and authentication host (Supabase) and push-notification delivery (Expo) — bound to process data only on our behalf. Otherwise we disclose data only if the law requires it or to protect members' safety.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my account permanently removes your profile, photos, matches, and messages — immediately from the live service, and from encrypted backups within 30 days. Already uninstalled the app? Email us and we'll do it for you. Full detail, and exactly what gets deleted, is on the account deletion page.

Age

40/LOVE is for adults 18 and older. We enforce this at signup and remove underage accounts when reported.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
