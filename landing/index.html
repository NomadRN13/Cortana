FAQ

Before you ask.

Is 40/Love only for dating?

No — dating is one of three modes. Play mode finds you hitting partners and Friends mode is for leagues and social play. Plenty of members will never use Date mode, and that's the plan.

Which sports are included?

Tennis, pickleball, padel, racquetball, and squash at launch. You can list more than one — most players do.

What skill level do I need?

Any. You set your level — from "started last month" to tournament ratings like NTRP or DUPR — and we match you with people who make for a fun, competitive-enough rally.

When does it launch?

40/Love is open in Indianapolis, Los Angeles, San Diego, Phoenix, Seattle, Spokane, Dallas, Houston, Orlando, Miami, and Washington, DC. You're matched with players in your own city — a dating app is only as good as who's actually near you. Not on the list? Vote above and we'll go where the players are; waitlist members hear first.

How much will it cost?

Free to join, match, and message. A premium tier with extras comes later — founding members get it free for 6 months.
