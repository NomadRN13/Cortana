Account deletion

Delete your account.

Applies to the 40/LOVE app (com.fortylove.app) and the 40love website

If you still have the app

This is instant and you don't need to contact anyone:

  1. Open 40/LOVE and go to Settings (the menu button, top right).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your profile disappears from everyone else's app immediately.

If you've already uninstalled it

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete the account for you. We reply to every request, and we'll confirm when it's done.

Email a deletion request

Or write to hello@40love.app yourself. Please send it from the address on the account — it's how we confirm the request is really yours, and we won't delete an account on someone else's say-so.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account. Deleting is permanent — there is no restore, and signing up again later creates a brand-new empty account.

DataWhat happens
ProfileName, birthdate, gender, sports, skill level, bio, availability and city — deleted.
PhotosDeleted, including the image files themselves, not just the links to them.
Email & phoneDeleted. Your phone number is not kept on any list.
Likes & passesDeleted.
MatchesDeleted. They disappear from your matches' apps too.
MessagesDeleted, on both sides of the conversation.
EventsYour RSVPs are removed.
NotificationsDeleted, along with the push token for every device you signed in on.
DevicesThe list of phones you've signed in on is deleted.
Reports & blocksDeleted, including reports you filed about other members.

How long it takes

The deletion itself is immediate: the moment you confirm, the data is gone from the live database and you vanish from other members' apps. Encrypted database backups are the only copies that persist, and those roll off within 30 days, after which no copy remains anywhere.

Deleting your account is not the same as deleting the app. Removing 40/LOVE from your phone leaves your profile visible to other members — use one of the two routes above.

Just want to take a break?

You don't have to delete anything. In Settings you can turn off any of the three modes (Date, Play, Friends), which takes you out of that deck, or sign out a device you no longer use. Your matches and messages stay where they are.
