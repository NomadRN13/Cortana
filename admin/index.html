Places to play

The courts members see under Events for their cities. Add one here and it is in the app the next time someone opens Events — no app update. Leave Courts blank and Lights on “Not sure” when you don't know: a blank is honest, a guess sends someone on a drive.

Tournaments

Single-elimination brackets for 2 to 72 entries. Create one and members enter from Events. When entries are in, Make the draw: the bracket is built for however many entered and everyone is seeded by a random draw. When the field isn't a power of two the top seeds get byes, so who gets one is the luck of the draw and they are spread through the bracket. Players record their own results; click a player here to record or correct one.
