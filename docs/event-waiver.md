By RSVPing to and attending an RSVPlay event, I confirm that:

1. **I am 18 or older**, and so is anyone playing on my profile as a doubles partner.
2. **I am playing at my own risk.** Racquet and paddle sports involve physical
   exertion and a risk of injury. I am fit to take part, I will stop if I am
   not, and I am responsible for my own equipment.
3. **The venue's rules apply.** Courts, hours, fees and facilities belong to
   the venue. RSVPlay organises the meetup; it does not own or supervise the
   courts unless the event says so.
4. **I will follow the code of conduct:** be on time, play the level I said I
   play, rotate when the format rotates, call lines honestly, and treat every
   player, host and venue staff member with respect. Harassment, threats, or
   unwanted contact get a player removed on the spot and reported to the desk.
5. **Photos.** Events may be photographed for RSVPlay's own channels. I can
   tell the host I do not want to be photographed and they will respect it.
6. **Hosts can remove a player** from an event for breaking these rules, and
   RSVPlay can suspend or ban an account.
7. **Meeting people is my choice.** RSVPlay does not run criminal background
   checks or verify identity beyond a phone number. I will meet in public
   places and use the in-app block and report tools if anything feels wrong.
