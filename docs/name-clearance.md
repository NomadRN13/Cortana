# Name clearance — "RSVPlay" (first pass, 2026-11-13)

**This is a web search, not a trademark clearance.** It can show an obvious
collision; it cannot show the absence of one. The USPTO register, domain
WHOIS and the social platforms were not reachable from this check and remain
to be done by the founder or counsel.

## What a web search found

| Name found | What it is | Risk to RSVPlay |
|---|---|---|
| No product, app, domain or handle called "RSVPlay" | Two searches (the name as an app; the name with trademark, `.com`, `.app`, `@rsvplay`) returned nothing by that exact name | Encouraging, not conclusive |
| **RSVPay** (RSVPay LLC, Google Play) | An event app for groups: plan events, collect payment up front, know who is coming | **The one to take to counsel.** One letter apart, and in the same business (event RSVPs). Likelihood-of-confusion is a legal judgment |
| RS-PLAY (RadioSparx) | Background-music streaming for businesses | Low: different field |
| RS PLAY (LogicaHost) | Brazilian TV listing app | Low |
| RSVP / RSVP.COM marks, RSVPify, rsvpful, RSVEP | Event-invitation and RSVP products built on the generic word "RSVP" | Low individually; shows "RSVP" itself is crowded and weak, so the distinctive part of the mark is the "Play" join |
