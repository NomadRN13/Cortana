# Proposal — billing plans and payments (2026-11-14)

**Status: waiting on the founder. Nothing in this document is built.**
Asked for on 2026-11-14: "add billing plans and forms of payment".

## Why this is a proposal and not a build

1. **It reverses a rule that never moves.** `CLAUDE.md` rule 4 and
   `docs/rules-and-stops.md` say: *no payments, no deposits, no pricing by
   gender.* Only the founder can change that, in writing.
2. **Three decisions change what gets built** (below). Building on a guess
   would be thrown away.
3. **It needs accounts only the founder can open** (Apple and Google
   agreements with banking and tax forms, a billing service, a card
   processor), and testing on real phones with the stores' test purchases.

## What already exists

- **Plans as entitlements.** `profiles.tier` is `free`, `plus` or `club`
  (migration 38). A member can never set their own tier; today only the desk
  can. Club members get early access to event signup.
- **Prices on events, display only.** An event can carry a price and a Club
  discount; the app shows them and takes no money.

So "billing plans" has a home already. What is missing is a way to pay and
a trusted way for a payment to set the tier.

## The stores decide the payment method, by what is sold

| What is sold | Apple and Google require | Forms of payment the member sees | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Membership** (Plus, Club): a digital benefit used in the app | Their own billing: Apple In-App Purchase, Google Play Billing | Whatever is on the member's Apple or Google account: cards, Apple Pay or Google Pay, PayPal, gift balance, carrier billing | 15% to the store under their small-business programmes (30% above $1M a year) |
| **A ticket to an in-person event**: a service used outside the app | A card processor is allowed, and expected | Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay | About 2.9% + 30¢ per charge (Stripe, US) |

Selling a membership in the app through a card form instead of the stores'
billing gets the app rejected. Apple's US rules on linking out to a website
to pay have loosened since 2025 and are still in court; this plan does not
depend on them.

## Recommended design

- **Memberships: RevenueCat** on top of Apple and Google billing. One
  integration for both stores; free at small volume, then about 1% of
  revenue. The stores handle renewals, refunds, receipts and tax collection.
  A payment sets the tier server-side: RevenueCat calls a Supabase function,
  which verifies the call and updates `profiles.tier`. The rule "a member
  cannot set their own tier" stays true, and stays tested.
- **Event tickets: Stripe.** The app opens Stripe's own payment sheet; card
  numbers never touch RSVPlay's app, website or database. A Supabase function
  creates the charge for the event's real price (never a price sent by the
  phone) and a Stripe webhook marks the RSVP paid.
- **RSVPlay stores:** which plan a member has, when it renews, and that an
  RSVP is paid. Never a card number.
- **Unchanged:** no pricing by gender, at any tier or event.

## Decisions for the founder

1. **What is sold first?** (a) Memberships only, (b) event tickets only,
   (c) both. *Recommendation: (a). It is one integration and the stores do
   the tax and refunds.*
2. **The plans and prices.** What Plus and Club cost per month and per year,
   and what each includes beyond early signup and the event discount. These
   are yours; nothing here assumes a number.
3. **When?** (a) Ship version 1.0 free now and add payments in 1.1, or
   (b) hold the store submission until payments work. *Recommendation: (a).
   1.0 is ready; payments add store agreements, bank and tax forms, purchase
   review and device testing, and a first review goes faster without them.*
4. **For paid events, who is paid?** RSVPlay, or the club or host running
   the event. If hosts are paid, it needs Stripe Connect and a host
   agreement. *Recommendation: RSVPlay only at first.*
5. **Refunds and cancellations.** Stores set the rules for memberships. For
   tickets: refundable until when? This also settles the old "no deposits"
   question: a ticket that is refundable until a cut-off is not a deposit; a
   fee kept from a no-show is.

## What the founder sets up (cannot be done from the repo)

- Apple: the Paid Apps agreement, bank account and tax forms in App Store
  Connect; subscription products and a subscription group.
- Google: a payments profile in Play Console; subscription products.
- RevenueCat: an account, both stores connected, one entitlement per plan.
- Stripe (only if tickets): an account with business verification.
- A decision on sales tax for tickets (Stripe Tax can calculate it; an
  accountant confirms where it is owed).

## What changes in the kit when approved

- **Rule 4** rewritten by the founder, for example: "Payments only through
  Apple and Google billing and Stripe; no card data stored; no fee kept from
  a no-show; no pricing by gender."
- A migration for subscription state and paid RSVPs, with row-level security
  and harness tests (a member cannot mark themselves paid or upgraded).
- Two Supabase functions (billing webhook, ticket charge) with signature
  checks.
- App: a plans screen, restore purchases, manage or cancel links, and the
  renewal wording Apple requires next to every subscribe button.
- Privacy policy, terms, the store privacy forms ("Purchases") and the
  content policy on the website (`landing/_headers`, which blocks payment
  today on purpose).
- `docs/security.md`: the card row changes from "no payments" to "held by
  the processor".

**Honest limit:** purchases can only be proven on real phones with the
stores' test accounts. Tests in this repository can cover the database rules
and the functions, not the purchase itself.
