# Indianapolis, IN — 40/LOVE Launch-City Target List

*Compiled by community-scout · August 2026 · every entry sourced; verify
contact routes before outreach.*

## Summary

Indianapolis is a genuinely strong launch city. The pickleball scene is one of
the best in the Midwest: **65+ public courts across Indy Parks**, at least
five dedicated indoor pickleball facilities (including a 24/7 club), and
national operators (The Picklr, Chicken N Pickle) actively investing in the
metro. Tennis is deep too — Greater Indianapolis is described as one of the
largest tennis markets in the Midwest with 50+ dedicated indoor courts, an
active USTA Central Indiana section, and social-first formats already running
(brewery-based "Tennis on Tap"). Indoor density largely de-risks winter.

**Three standout opportunities:**
1. **Indy Pickleball Club** — a nonprofit whose stated mission is growing
   pickleball in Central Indiana, with three indoor locations. Mission-aligned
   mixer co-host; one yes here unlocks a community, not just a venue.
2. **Chicken N Pickle (Fishers District)** — indoor/outdoor courts plus a
   restaurant, purpose-built for social events and already running community
   programming. The ideal 40/LOVE Social Mixer venue.
3. **USTA Central Indiana's "Tennis on Tap"** — social Red Ball tennis at
   breweries. This is our exact audience being gathered by someone else;
   partner, don't compete.

**Gap worth knowing:** Indiana currently has **no padel facilities** (per
padel directories) — lead with pickleball + tennis in Indy; padel is a
later-city sport.

7. **West Indy Racquet Club** — connector staff; ask for intros as much as courts.
8. **Indy Parks permits office** — permit path for Riverside/Ellenberger/Tarkington public mixers.
9. **JCC Indianapolis** — established community programming; low-pressure beginner events.
