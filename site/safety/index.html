Safety

Play it safe.

November 2026 · For everyone meeting through RSVPlay

RSVPlay does not run criminal background checks or identity checks. A verified phone number means a member has a working phone, not that we know who they are. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

Before you meet

  • Keep chatting in the app until you're comfortable. Don't hand over your phone number, address, workplace or social accounts early.
  • Look them up. A quick search of their name and photos can catch a fake profile.
  • Never send money, gift cards or financial details to anyone you meet here, whatever the story. Report anyone who asks.
  • A video call first is a good way to check someone is who they say they are.

Meeting on court

  • Meet at public courts in daylight or under good lights, with other people around, for the first few times.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, who you're meeting, and when you'll be back.
  • Get yourself there and back. Don't accept a ride from someone you've just met, or let them follow you home.
  • Keep your phone charged and your bag with you.
  • Leave whenever you want. You never owe anyone another set.

Looking after your body

  • Warm up, bring water, and play at a level that suits you.
  • If you have a heart, joint or other health condition, check with a doctor before you take up a new sport or step up how much you play.
  • Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or a sharp pain, and get help.

Reporting and blocking

Every profile and chat has a Report and Block option. A block is absolute: neither of you sees the other again, including in tournament entry lists. Reports are confidential — the person you report is not told who reported them. We aim to review every report within 24 hours. You can also email hello@rsvplayapp.com.

If something happened in person, report it to the police too. Tell them you met through RSVPlay; we will cooperate with them.

Where to get help

  • Emergency: 911
  • RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline: 1-800-656-4673, or rainn.org
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988
  • Scams: report to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov
