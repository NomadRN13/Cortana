03How it's underwritten

Every farm is priced like a loan, not a hope.

Each community is underwritten bottom-up. Nothing is assumed that MIBOR data can't verify — and the one dominant competitor is subtracted out before we count a single winnable listing.

Cost of touches

Homes × 12 mailings × $0.65 = mail budget. No guesswork on spend.

Winnable pool

Annual sales × (1 − dominant-agent share). We never count listings a saturated competitor already owns.

Realistic capture

A modeled 15% of the winnable pool — against a break-even of under 1%.

Underwritten GCI

Captured × median price × 2.82% = gross commission, then net of mail.

Conservative by design

Medians seeded below small-sample Redfin highs; luxury turnover held at 4%.

Saturation flagged, not hidden

Any farm a single agent dominates is auto-flagged saturated until the data clears it.

Verified to the dollar

The two anchor farms below reconcile exactly to this model — the math is auditable.

Assumptions & disclosures

Net basis. "Net" = gross commission less mail cost — channel contribution before brokerage split, taxes, and other business costs. Both the Zillow and farm figures are stated on this same basis, so the swing is like-for-like. A take-home / DSCR view follows from your commission plan.

Verified vs. seeded. Hamilton Proper and Gray Eagle are fully modeled and reconcile to the dollar. The other four farms (Britton Falls, Sunblest, Geist, Windermere) use seeded estimates pending the MIBOR pull; shown only in combined form, reconciled to portfolio totals.

Conservatism. Medians seeded below small-sample Redfin highs; luxury turnover held at 4%; 15% capture applied to the pool net of the dominant agent — and stress-tested down to 5%. Modeled potential at full ramp, not guaranteed results.
