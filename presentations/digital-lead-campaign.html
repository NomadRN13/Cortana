Assumptions & disclosures

Dashboard vs. modeled. Connections, additional leads, share of voice and per-ZIP budgets are pulled from the live campaign dashboard. Close rate (2%) and commission (2.82%) are modeled assumptions.

Net basis. “Net” = gross commission less ad spend — before brokerage split, taxes and other business costs. A take-home / DSCR view follows from your commission plan.

Conservatism. 2% close is at the low end for internet real-estate leads; “<1” dashboard values are counted as ~0.5. Break-even sits at a 1.5% close. Modeled potential, not guaranteed results.
